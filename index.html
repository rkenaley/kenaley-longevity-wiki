The Epistemic Graveyard: Retired & Deprecated Interventions

Ruthless pruning of hypotheses when empirical human trials or biophysical realities contradict theoretical enthusiasm.

RET-01

Chronic Daily Metformin in Resistance Lifters

Cause of Termination: MASTERS Trial (Walton et al., Aging Cell 2019) demonstrated 30-50% blunting of muscle hypertrophy and impaired mitochondrial respiration. Replaced with Acarbose (Module 01) which acts purely in gut lumen.

RET-02

Dasatinib + Quercetin (D+Q) Senolytics

Cause of Termination: Off-target hematological toxicities, pleural effusions, and pulmonary arterial hypertension risks from dasatinib tyrosine kinase inhibition. Replaced with Mayo Pulsed Fisetin (Module 14).

RET-03

Continuous C60 Fullerene in Olive Oil (Baati Protocol)

Cause of Termination: Independent replication failure (Grohn et al., 2021) and light-induced phototoxicity generating cytotoxic epoxides with indefinite tissue bioaccumulation. Replaced by SS-31 and DLPC (Modules 04, 35).

RET-04

Supraphysiological TRT & Continuous rhGH

Cause of Termination: Complete HPG axis shutdown, elevated hematocrit/thrombotic risks, insulin resistance, and oncogenic transformation risks. Replaced with Enclomiphene microdosing (Module 17) and pulsatile GHRH/GHRP secretagogues (Module 19).

RET-05

Chronic High-Dose Non-Selective Antioxidants Post-Workout

Cause of Termination: Landmark trials (Ristow et al., PNAS 2009; Paulsen et al., 2014) proved post-workout mega-dose Vitamin C & E blunts PGC-1alpha and exercise adaptations. Replaced by strict timing separation and PQQ (Module 05).
