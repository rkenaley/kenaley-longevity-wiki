## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Microbiome-derived metabolite of ellagitannins (from pomegranates and walnuts). Crosses cellular membranes to activate PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy, clearing depolarized mitochondria and stimulating skeletal muscle mitochondrial volume density.
