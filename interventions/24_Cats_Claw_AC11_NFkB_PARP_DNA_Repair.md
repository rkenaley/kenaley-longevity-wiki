## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Standardized aqueous extract of Uncaria tomentosa rich in Carboxy Alkyl Esters (CAEs). Inhibits NF-kappaB nuclear translocation by preventing IkappaBalpha phosphorylation. Upregulates Poly(ADP-Ribose) Polymerase (PARP) activity and stimulates Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER) of DNA lesions.

Sheng, Pero et al. (Phytomedicine 2000, 2001): Human clinical trials demonstrated that oral AC-11 significantly enhanced the repair of single- and double-strand DNA damage induced by radiation/chemotherapy and normalized leukocyte survival.
