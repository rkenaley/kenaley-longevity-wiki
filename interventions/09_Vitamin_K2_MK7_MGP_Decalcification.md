## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Acts as obligatory enzymatic cofactor for gamma-glutamyl carboxylase, transforming uncarboxylated Matrix Gla Protein (ucMGP) into carboxylated MGP (cMGP). Active cMGP chelates free calcium phosphate in the vascular media and intima, preventing medial elastocalcinosis and aortic stiffness.

---

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Rotterdam Study (Geleijnse 2004): High dietary menaquinone intake correlated with 52% reduction in severe aortic calcification and 57% lower coronary mortality. VitaK-CAC Trial (Zwakenberg 2021) demonstrated arrest of arterial pulse wave velocity acceleration.

## 4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

## 5. Precise Operational Protocol
100mcg to 200mcg Menaquinone-7 (MK-7) daily taken with a fat-containing meal (prolonged circulatory half-life of ~72 hours).
