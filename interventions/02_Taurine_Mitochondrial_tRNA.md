## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Serves as substrate for MTO1/GTPBP3 mitochondrial tRNA-Leu taurinomethylation at nucleotide 34, forming 5-taurinomethyluridine (tau-m5-U). Stabilizes UUG codon translation of 13 mtDNA-encoded hydrophobic Complex I subunits (ND6, ND1). Stabilizes SERCA2a calcium ATPase in muscle, acts as intracellular osmolyte, and conjugates secondary bile acids (taurocholate) activating TGR5.

---

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Singh et al. (Science 2023): Circulating taurine declines >80% with age in humans and primates. Oral supplementation in mice increased median lifespan by 10-12% and life expectancy at 28 months by 18-25%. Improved bone mineral density, muscle endurance, and reduced cellular senescence.
