## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Uncompetitive inhibitor of Glycogen Synthase Kinase-3 Beta (GSK-3beta). Hyperactive GSK-3beta drives tau hyperphosphorylation and amyloid-beta synthesis. Microdose lithium stimulates hippocampal BDNF transcription, promotes neurogenesis, and preserves cortical gray matter volume.

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Large prospective epidemiological studies across Austria, Japan, and Denmark demonstrating that trace lithium concentrations in municipal drinking water correlate with statistically significant reductions in dementia and all-cause mortality.
