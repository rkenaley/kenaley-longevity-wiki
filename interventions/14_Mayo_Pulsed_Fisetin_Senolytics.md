## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Selectively downregulates pro-survival BCL-2, BCL-xL, and Akt in senescent cells, tipping the intracellular rheostat toward Bad/Bax-mediated caspase-3/7 cleavage and apoptosis. Clears senescent preadipocytes and endothelial cells while sparing healthy somatic tissue.

---

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Yousefzadeh et al. (EBioMedicine 2018): Identified fisetin as the most potent natural senolytic among 10 flavonoids screened; restored tissue homeostasis and extended median and maximum lifespan in aged mice. Currently in ongoing Mayo Clinic clinical trials (AFFIRM-LATOR).

---

## 3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value
* **P(Real Human Healthspan Extension):** **50%**
* **Expected Value Justification:** The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

---

## 4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions
Poor oral bioavailability and rapid hepatic glucuronidation unless co-ingested with dietary lipids or formulated in phytosomes. Transient mild fatigue on dosing days due to apoptotic cellular debris clearance.

---

## 5. Precise Operational Protocol
20mg to 25mg per kg body weight (~1500-1800mg) taken once daily for 2 consecutive days, repeated once monthly for 2-3 months, followed by a multi-month washout. Must be taken with 20-30mL extra virgin olive oil.

---

## 6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria
* **Biomarkers to Monitor:** Failure to reduce circulating SASP markers (IL-6, TNF-alpha) or worsening of epigenetic pace of aging (DunedinPACE).
* **Kill Rule:** If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
