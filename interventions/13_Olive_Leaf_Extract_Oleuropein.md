## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Rich in the secoiridoid glycoside Oleuropein and its metabolite Hydroxytyrosol. Chelates catalytic free iron and copper ions in circulating lipoproteins, preventing the lipid peroxidation that transforms ApoB into atherogenic oxLDL. Stimulates eNOS phosphorylation and acts as a natural ACE-inhibitor.

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Susalit et al. (Phytomedicine 2011): 500mg standardized extract twice daily reduced systolic blood pressure by -11.5 mmHg and diastolic by -4.8 mmHg in stage-1 hypertensive patients, matching 10mg Captopril head-to-head.
