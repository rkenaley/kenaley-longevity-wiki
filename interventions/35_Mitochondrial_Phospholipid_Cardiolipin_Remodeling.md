### A. The Three-Way Collision Between NAD+ Precursors (`03`), Methylation (`23`), and Mitochondrial Membranes
1. **The PEMT Methyl Drain:** While 70% of cellular PC is synthesized via the CDP-choline (Kennedy) pathway (`26`), **30% of hepatic and mitochondrial-associated ER membrane (MAM) Phosphatidylcholine** is synthesized by **Phosphatidylethanolamine N-methyltransferase (PEMT)**, which consumes **three molecules of S-Adenosylmethionine (SAMe)** per molecule of PE converted to PC:
   $$\text{Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE)} + 3\,\text{SAMe} \xrightarrow{\text{PEMT}} \text{Phosphatidylcholine (PC)} + 3\,\text{SAH}$$
2. **The GAMT & NNMT Competition:** Endogenous synthesis of creatine by **guanidinoacetate N-methyltransferase (GAMT)** consumes ~45% of total systemic SAMe, while unbuffered Nicotinamide in `03_Turnbuckle_IMM_Latching_Dischler` induces **nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT)**, draining the remaining SAMe pool and raising S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH)—a potent competitive inhibitor of both **PEMT** and **DNMT3a/b** epigenetic maintenance!
3. **The Tafazzin Cardiolipin Remodeling Bottleneck:** Nascent cardiolipin in the IMM contains saturated and monounsaturated fatty acyl chains with poor packing geometry. The transacylase **Tafazzin (TAZ)** remodels nascent cardiolipin into mature **Tetralinoleoyl Cardiolipin ($\text{L}_4\text{CL}$, containing four $18:2\text{n-}6$ linoleoyl chains)** by transferring linoleoyl groups directly from **1,2-dilinoleoylphosphatidylcholine (DLPC, $18:2/18:2$-PC)**:
   $$\text{Monolysocardiolipin (MLCL)} + \text{DLPC } (18:2\text{-PC}) \stackrel{\text{Tafazzin (TAZ)}}{\rightleftharpoons} \text{Tetralinoleoyl Cardiolipin } (\text{L}_4\text{CL}) + \text{LPC}$$
   With age, **ALCAT1** pathologically substitutes peroxidizable docosahexaenoic acid ($22:6\text{n-}3$ DHA) into cardiolipin, sensitizing the IMM to lipid peroxidation unless sufficient **DLPC** substrate is supplied for Tafazzin.
