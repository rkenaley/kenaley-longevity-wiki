## 1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism
Natural polyamine required for the deoxyhypusine synthase-mediated hypusination of eukaryotic translation initiation factor 5A (eIF5A). Hypusinated eIF5A is obligatory for translating polyproline-rich motifs, including Transcription Factor EB (TFEB), the master regulator of lysosomal biogenesis.

## 2. The Empirical Evidence Base
Kiechl et al. (Am J Clin Nutr 2018): High dietary spermidine intake in the prospective Bruneck cohort (n=829) was independently associated with significantly reduced all-cause mortality (hazard ratio 0.68). Murine trials show lifespan extension.
