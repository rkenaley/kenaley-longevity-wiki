Microdose Tadalafil: Continuous PDE5 Inhibition & Endothelial Shear Protection

Pillar: Cardiovascular Longevity & Endothelial Glycocalyx
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E2 (Human Endothelial Biomarkers) / E1 (BPH / LUTS)
Stance: Contrarian (Daily low-dose vascular prophylaxis vs on-demand ED use)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $15 -- $20 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Altered Intercellular Communication & Mitochondrial Dysfunction

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Continuous inhibition of Phosphodiesterase Type 5 (PDE5) prevents the hydrolysis of cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), prolonging nitric oxide (NO) signaling. Upregulates eNOS phosphorylation at Ser1177, reduces arterial pulse wave velocity, shields the endothelial glycocalyx from oxidative shear, and improves pelvic perfusion.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Extensive clinical trial data demonstrating systemic blood pressure reduction, improved flow-mediated dilation (FMD), lower rates of major adverse cardiovascular events in diabetic cohorts, and resolution of lower urinary tract symptoms.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 80%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Absolute contraindication with organic nitrates (nitroglycerin). Rare transient headache or dyspepsia during initiation.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

2.5mg to 5.0mg daily taken every morning or evening (prolonged 17.5-hour half-life maintains steady-state 24-hour cGMP elevation).

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Refractory hypotension (systolic BP < 95 mmHg), visual disturbances, or priapism.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
