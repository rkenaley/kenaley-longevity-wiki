1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

In May 2026, Poliezhaieva & Ermolaeva et al. (Nature Communications, 2026; Leibniz Institute on Aging) demonstrated that age-dependent mitochondrial fragmentation and bioenergetic collapse are driven by the progressive depletion of mitochondrial membrane Phosphatidylcholine (PC) and age-related dysregulation of S-adenosylmethionine synthetase (SAMS-1 / MAT2A).

A. The Three-Way Collision Between NAD+ Precursors (03), Methylation (23), and Mitochondrial Membranes

  1. The PEMT Methyl Drain: While 70% of cellular PC is synthesized via the CDP-choline (Kennedy) pathway (26), 30% of hepatic and mitochondrial-associated ER membrane (MAM) Phosphatidylcholine is synthesized by Phosphatidylethanolamine N-methyltransferase (PEMT), which consumes three molecules of S-Adenosylmethionine (SAMe) per molecule of PE converted to PC:
  2. \text{Phosphatidylethanolamine (PE)} + 3\,\text{SAMe} \xrightarrow{\text{PEMT}} \text{Phosphatidylcholine (PC)} + 3\,\text{SAH}
  3. The GAMT & NNMT Competition: Endogenous synthesis of creatine by guanidinoacetate N-methyltransferase (GAMT) consumes ~45% of total systemic SAMe, while unbuffered Nicotinamide in 03_Turnbuckle_IMM_Latching_Dischler induces nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT), draining the remaining SAMe pool and raising S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH)—a potent competitive inhibitor of both PEMT and DNMT3a/b epigenetic maintenance!
  4. The Tafazzin Cardiolipin Remodeling Bottleneck: Nascent cardiolipin in the IMM contains saturated and monounsaturated fatty acyl chains with poor packing geometry. The transacylase Tafazzin (TAZ) remodels nascent cardiolipin into mature Tetralinoleoyl Cardiolipin (\text{L}_4\text{CL}, containing four $18:2\text{n-}6 linoleoyl chains) by transferring linoleoyl groups directly from 1,2-dilinoleoylphosphatidylcholine (DLPC, $18:2/18:2-PC):
  5. \text{Monolysocardiolipin (MLCL)} + \text{DLPC } (18:2\text{-PC}) \stackrel{\text{Tafazzin (TAZ)}}{\rightleftharpoons} \text{Tetralinoleoyl Cardiolipin } (\text{L}_4\text{CL}) + \text{LPC}
  6. With age, ALCAT1 pathologically substitutes peroxidizable docosahexaenoic acid ($22:6\text{n-}3 DHA) into cardiolipin, sensitizing the IMM to lipid peroxidation unless sufficient DLPC substrate is supplied for Tafazzin.

  • Brosnan et al. (Biochim Biophys Acta 2011): Creatine supplementation reduces endogenous GAMT methylation burden, sparing systemic methyl donors.
