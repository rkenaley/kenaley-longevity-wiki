1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Two of the 19 biomarkers (10.5% of total phenotypic weight) are direct oral health metrics (Periodontal Attachment Loss and Dental Caries), while two additional metrics (hs-CRP and Total WBC/Neutrophil Count, another 10.5% of clock weight) are directly perturbed by subgingival translocation of red-complex periodontal pathogens (Porphyromonas gingivalis, Tannerella forsythia, Treponema denticola, and Fusobacterium nucleatum).

A. The Gingipain-Inflammaging-Neurodegeneration Axis

P. gingivalis* secretes cysteine proteases (gingipains RgpA, RgpB, and Kgp) and lipopolysaccharide (LPS) that enter systemic circulation during mastication, activating endothelial and microglial TLR2/TLR4 \to NF-\kappaB and NLRP3 inflammasomes, cleaving apolipoprotein E (ApoE), and driving hepatic hs-CRP elevation (>0.5\text{ mg/L}).
