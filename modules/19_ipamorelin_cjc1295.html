1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Ipamorelin selectively agonizes GHSR-1a without elevating ACTH, cortisol, prolactin, or ghrelin. CJC-1295 No-DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) agonizes the GHRH receptor. Together, they amplify the amplitude of nocturnal slow-wave sleep growth hormone secretory bursts.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

FDA investigational monographs and endocrine literature proving nocturnal pulsatile GH release, connective tissue collagen synthesis, and thymic fat reduction without inducing chronic daytime insulin resistance.

  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Must be taken in a completely fasted state (insulin blunts GH release). Continuous GH hyper-stimulation accelerates aging in Ames/Laron dwarf models; pulsatile 5on/2off dosing avoids this hazard.

  • Primary Protocol (Pulsatile SubQ): 100–200 mcg Ipamorelin + 100 mcg CJC-1295 No-DAC injected subcutaneously immediately before bed, strictly 5 nights on / 2 nights off, in a minimum 3-hour postprandial fasted state.

  • Context:* When traveling internationally, flying through customs without refrigerated peptide transport, or during off-cycle peptide washout periods.
  • Dosing:* 5.0 g to 8.0 g pure L-Ornithine powder + 250–500 mg Choline Bitartrate / Alpha-GPC dissolved in 8 oz cold RO water, ingested 30 minutes before sleep on an empty stomach (3+ hours post-dinner).
  • Mechanism: Discovered empirically by Paul Kiesow (1998 Life Extension* protocol), oral L-ornithine enhances nocturnal growth hormone release by suppressing hypothalamic somatostatin tone, clearing hepatic ammonia, and promoting GABAergic slow-wave sleep.
