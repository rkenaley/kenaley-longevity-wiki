1. Executive Summary & Systems Objective

  • Primary Objective: Maximize the myotrophic therapeutic index (net retainable contractile tissue and peak neuromuscular force output per unit of HPTA suppression, dyslipidemia, and hepatotoxicity).
  • Core Pharmacological Lever: AC-262536 (4-[(1R,3r,5S)-3-endo-hydroxy-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octan-8-yl]naphthalene-1-carbonitrile), operating as a high-affinity, non-steroidal partial androgen receptor (AR) agonist (K_i \approx 5.01\text{ nM}, E_{\max} \approx 66\text{--}70\%, ED_{50}\text{ Myotrophic:Androgenic Ratio} \approx 14:1).
  • Chronobiological Strategy: Exploit AC-262’s lack of metabolic-blocking halogens and rapid Phase II O-glucuronidation (T_{\max} \approx 1\text{--}2\text{ hr}, t_{1/2} \approx 6\text{--}12\text{ hr}) by executing a morning/pre-workout pulse. This aligns peak plasma free ligand (C_{\max}) with mechanotransduction and cholinergic/dopaminergic motor unit recruitment while achieving a $75\text{--}87\% systemic clearance prior to the nocturnal hypothalamic GnRH/LH secretory window (02:00–08:00).

2. Receptor Biophysics & Myostatin/Follistatin Dynamics

2.1 Structural Basis of Partial Agonism

  • A-Ring Mimic: The C1 nitrile (-\text{C}\equiv\text{N}) on the naphthalene ring forms electrostatic hydrogen bonds with Arg752 and Gln711 inside the AR ligand-binding pocket, while the planar \pi-system engages Met745, Leu704, and Phe764.
  • Helix-12 Steric Perturbation: The C4-linked 8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane (tropane) core projects a stereospecific 3-endo-hydroxyl toward Asn705 and Thr877, while the rigid two-carbon ethylene bridge introduces localized steric bulk against the Helix-12 (AF-2) folding lid.
  • Coactivator Selectivity: Prevention of full Helix-12 closure impairs canonical N/C-terminal (AF-1/AF-2) dimerization. This blunts prostate and central neuroendocrine transcriptional complexes while retaining high myotrophic transcription in skeletal muscle (where baseline AR occupancy is sub-saturating and $5\alpha-reductase activity is minimal).

2.2 The Myostatin (MSTN) / Follistatin (FST) Conformational Loophole

  • Bypassing the Canonical MSTN Brake: Full AR agonists (Testosterone, DHT, RAD-140) induce tight N/C-terminal AR dimerization, binding canonical Androgen Response Elements (AREs) on the mammalian MSTN promoter to upregulate myostatin mRNA as a homeostatic growth brake around week 6–8. Because AC-262’s tropane bridge sterically restricts full AF-1/AF-2 closure, canonical ARE-driven MSTN transactivation is substantially attenuated.
  • Preserving Non-Canonical Follistatin Signaling: Liganded AR translocation still tethers cytoplasmic \beta-catenin and shuttles it to TCF4 nuclear sites to upregulate Follistatin (FST) while driving PI3K/Akt-mediated phosphorylation and cytoplasmic sequestration of FOXO3a (preventing catabolic MSTN transcription).

3. Core Protocol Modifications & Force Multipliers

3.1 [PROMOTED] Geranylgeraniol (GG) — Primary Anabolic & Mevalonate Amplifier

Geranylgeraniol is elevated from a passive mitochondrial/statin-support adjunct to a Tier-1 myotrophic and endocrine force multiplier:
1. RhoA Prenylation & Sarcolemmal Mechanotransduction: Endogenous geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate (GGPP) is the obligatory lipid donor for the post-translational geranylgeranylation (prenylation) of Rho-family small GTPases (RhoA, Rac1, Cdc42). Prenylated RhoA anchors to the sarcolemma to transduce mechanical tension into actin stress fiber polymerization, Focal Adhesion Kinase (FAK) activation, and nuclear translocation of YAP/TAZ. Nuclear YAP/TAZ works in direct transcriptional synergy with Ser81-phosphorylated, AC-262-bound AR to drive myofibrillar protein synthesis (MPS).
2. Atrogene Suppression: GG directly downregulates ubiquitin-proteasome E3 ligases Atrogin-1 (FBXO32) and MuRF1 (TRIM63), protecting fast-twitch (Type IIa/IIx) fibers from proteolytic turnover during high-output conditioning or caloric deficits.
3. Testicular Steroidogenic Amplification: GG upregulates StAR (Steroidogenic Acute Regulatory protein) and stimulates adenylate cyclase/cAMP/PKA signaling in Leydig cells, increasing intratesticular conversion of cholesterol to pregnenolone/testosterone in response to Enclomiphene-driven LH pulses.
4. Endogenous CoQ10 & MK-4 Tail Synthesis: Supplies the isoprenoid side-chain precursor required for endogenous synthesis of Ubiquinone (CoQ10) and tissue-specific conversion of phylloquinone/menadione into Menaquinone-4 (MK-4).

3.2 [ADDED] Pre-Workout Neuromuscular Potentiation: Piracetam + Alpha-GPC

Added to the pre-workout AC-262 pulse to maximize central motor cortex drive, corticospinal rate coding, and neuromuscular junction (NMJ) contraction force:
1. Alpha-GPC (300–600 mg) — Quantal ACh Release & Motor Unit Recruitment:
* Supplies ~40% elemental choline across the blood-brain barrier and directly to peripheral \alpha-motor neuron terminals.
* Prevents exercise-induced depletion of pre-synaptic acetylcholine (ACh) vesicles at the neuromuscular junction endplate during high-threshold isometric and concentric contractions, sustaining peak motor unit firing rates and potentiating acute exercise-induced pituitary Growth Hormone (GH) release.
2. Piracetam (1,600–2,400 mg) — AMPAkine Allosteric Modulation & Membrane Fluidity:
* Acts as a positive allosteric modulator (PAM) of central AMPA-type glutamate receptors, enhancing fast excitatory synaptic transmission, \text{Na}^+/\text{Ca}^{2+} flux, and interhemispheric motor cortex synchronization.
* Interacts with phospholipid polar head groups to increase neuronal and sarcolemmal membrane fluidity.
* Obligatory Pairing Rule: Piracetam accelerates hippocampal and cortical ACh turnover and upregulates muscarinic/nicotinic receptor density; co-administration with Alpha-GPC prevents choline depletion and maximizes synergistic force production alongside Selegiline + L-Tyrosine (dopaminergic drive) and AC-262 (acute non-genomic AR-mediated ERK1/2 and intracellular \text{Ca}^{2+} mobilization).

3.3 [DEMOTED] Curcumin — Restricted to Occasional / PRN Use Only

Curcumin (especially formulations containing piperine) is removed from the daily baseline stack and demoted to strictly occasional/PRN recovery use:
1. Eliminating the UGT/CYP Clearance Bottleneck: AC-262 relies on rapid hepatic Phase I oxidation/epimerization (3-endo-OH \rightarrow 3-ketone \rightarrow 3-exo-OH) followed by immediate Phase II glucuronidation via UGT2B17 and UGT2B7. Curcuminoids and piperine potently inhibit UGT2B17, UGT2B7, UGT1A1, CYP3A4, and P-glycoprotein (ABCB1). Daily dosing extends AC-262’s effective half-life from ~8 hours to >16–20 hours, ruining the diurnal pulse and causing systemic rollover into the nocturnal GnRH/LH window.
2. Preserving Myogenic Hormesis: Routine high-dose COX-2/NF-\kappaB blockade blunts acute post-exercise prostaglandin (\text{PGE}_2/\text{PGF}_{2\alpha}) and myokine (IL-6) signaling required for satellite cell chemotaxis and myonuclear addition.
3. Deployment Rule when Used PRN: Only deploy on non-training days or for acute systemic/joint inflammation flares, and separate by at least 10 hours from any morning AC-262 dose (preferring piperine-free phytosome/liposomal forms if used on-cycle).

3.4 Endocrine Stack Calibration (Enclomiphene, Exemestane, Boron, DHEA)

  • Enclomiphene Citrate (3.125–6.25 mg EOD or daily, Evening): Competitively antagonizes hypothalamic and pituitary ER\alpha, elevating GnRH/LH/FSH and keeping endogenous Total Testosterone in the upper quartile ($750\text{--}1,100\text{ ng/dL}) while AC-262 competitively shields the prostate and scalp from the resulting DHT surge.
  • Exemestane (6.25 mg — Strictly PRN Reserve Only): Do not run prophylactically. AC-262 does not aromatize, and high-dose Grape Seed Extract (>1.0\text{ g/day}) already provides mild competitive peripheral aromatase (CYP19A1) buffering. Deploying a suicidal AI alongside Enclomiphene risks dual estrogen deprivation (ligand depletion + ER\alpha blockade), which degrades eNOS, tendon collagen, HDL-C, and hepatic IGF-1. Deploy 6.25 mg only if LC-MS/MS bloodwork confirms E_2 > 55\text{--}60\text{ pg/mL} with clinical symptoms.
  • Boron (PAUSED ON-CYCLE): AC-262 reduces hepatic SHBG by 20–35% via first-pass hepatic AR activation. Pausing Boron prevents crashing SHBG below $12\text{--}15\text{ nmol/L} (which would accelerate testosterone metabolic clearance and spike peripheral aromatization).
  • DHEA (REDUCED TO 10–15 mg/day OR PAUSED): High-dose oral DHEA ($25\text{--}50\text{ mg}) converts rapidly to estrone/estradiol via hepatic $3\beta-HSD/$17\beta-HSD when intratesticular T is already elevated by Enclomiphene. Pregnenolone (10–30 mg evening) remains at full dose to preserve neurosteroid/allopregnanolone \text{GABA}_\text{A}$ tone.

5. Biomarker Verification & Off-Cycle Transition Protocol

5.1 Mid-Cycle Bloodwork Verification (Week 4–5, Fasted Morning Draw prior to AC-262 Pulse)

  • Endocrine Panel: Total Testosterone (LC-MS/MS), Free Testosterone (Equilibrium Dialysis), Estradiol (Ultra-Sensitive LC-MS/MS — target 25–45 pg/mL), LH, FSH, SHBG (target >15 nmol/L), IGF-1, DHEA-S.
  • Cardiometabolic & Rheology: ApoB, ApoA-I, HDL-C, Triglycerides, hs-CRP, Complete Blood Count (Monitor Hematocrit/Hemoglobin).
  • Hepatic & Renal: ALT, AST, GGT (specifically tracks biliary/canalicular stress), Total Bilirubin, Cystatin-C (essential because Creatine + increased muscle mass + SARM inhibition of renal OCT2 tubular creatinine secretion falsely elevates standard serum creatinine/eGFR).

5.2 Post-Cycle Transition (4-Week Washout & Consolidation)

  1. Day 1 Post-AC-262: Discontinue AC-262. Because HPTA pulsatility was preserved via morning pulsing + nocturnal low-dose Enclomiphene, there is no hypogonadal trough.
  2. Weeks 1–2 Post-Cycle:
  3. Taper Enclomiphene to 3.125 mg EOD for 10–14 days, then discontinue.
  4. Maintain Promoted GG (150–300 mg/day) + Ipamorelin + Creatine + Piracetam/Alpha-GPC: Sustaining RhoA/YAP-TAZ mechanotransduction, GH/IGF-1 signaling, and peak neural contraction force during the first 14 days off-cycle prevents post-cycle myonuclear loss.
  5. Reintroduce Boron (6–9 mg/day) at Day 4 post-AC-262 as hepatic SHBG rebounds from the cessation of oral AR stimulation.
  6. Restore DHEA back to baseline (25–50 mg/day) once Enclomiphene is discontinued at the end of Week 2.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Surveillance Biomarkers: Fasted morning pre-pulse: Total Testosterone (LC-MS/MS), Free Testosterone, Sensitive Estradiol (25–45 pg/mL), LH, FSH, SHBG (>15 nmol/L), ApoB, ApoA-I, HDL-C, ALT, AST, GGT, Cystatin-C.
  • Kill Criteria: ALT/AST elevation > 3× ULN, persistent suppression of LH (< 1.0 IU/L) despite Enclomiphene, profound HDL-C collapse (< 25 mg/dL), or severe dyslipidemia refractory to Citrus Bergamot.
