1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Photons in the red (660nm) and near-infrared (850nm) spectra penetrate tissue to be selectively absorbed by Cytochrome c Oxidase (CCO / Complex IV). Displaces inhibitory nitric oxide from CCO, restoring inner membrane potential, accelerating ATP generation, and triggering light-driven repair.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Sommer et al. (2009): Demonstrated accelerated cellular repair; clinical trials in sports medicine proving accelerated muscle recovery and reduced post-exercise creatine kinase leakage.

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 70%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Negligible adverse effects; avoid direct unshielded retinal exposure with high-power industrial LEDs. Non-thermal and non-ionizing.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

10 to 15 minutes of full-body exposure at 6-12 inches distance from a high-irradiance panel (>100 mW/cm^2) in the morning.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Lack of subjective recovery benefit or lack of improvement in skin elasticity.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
