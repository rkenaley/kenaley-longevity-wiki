Antarctic Krill Oil: Phospholipid EPA/DHA, MFSD2A Transport & Astaxanthin

Pillar: Cardiovascular Longevity & Neurobiology
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E2 (Human Pharmacokinetic & Biomarker Trials)
Stance: Contrarian on Form (Phospholipids vs standard triglyceride/ethyl ester fish oil)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $20 -- $30 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Loss of Proteostasis & Altered Intercellular Communication

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Delivers EPA and DHA bound to phosphatidylcholine (PC) phospholipids. PC-bound DHA crosses the blood-brain barrier via the MFSD2A transporter as lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC-DHA). Intrinsic marine astaxanthin anchors across the entire bilayer, quenching singlet oxygen and preventing lipid peroxidation.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Schuchardt et al. (Lipids Health Dis 2011): Demonstrated superior incorporation of EPA/DHA into plasma phospholipids compared to fish oil. Nguyen et al. (Nature 2014): Identified MFSD2A as the primary BBB DHA transporter requiring phospholipid conjugation.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 80%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

High doses of pure EPA/DHA (>4g daily) carry modest risks of atrial fibrillation; 1-2g krill oil maintains safe physiological levels without arrhythmia risk. Shellfish allergy contraindication.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

1000mg to 2000mg daily standardized Antarctic Krill Oil taken with midday or evening meal.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Onset of atrial fibrillation or failure to achieve an Omega-3 Index >8%.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
