Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT): Hyperoxic-Hypoxic Paradox & Stem Cells

Pillar: Biophysical Hardware & Regenerative Medicine
Conviction Tier: TESTING / EXPERIMENTAL CYCLE
Evidence Grade: E2 (Human Clinical Telomere/Stem Cell RCTs)
Stance: Contrarian (Deploying 2.0 ATA clinical hyperbarics for longevity vs wound care)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $120 -- $180 / month (amortized / clinic series)
Primary Target Hallmark: Telomere Attrition, Stem Cell Exhaustion & Cellular Senescence

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Breathing 100% oxygen at 2.0 ATA dissolves supraphysiological oxygen into plasma. Periodic 5-minute air breaks induce the Hyperoxic-Hypoxic Paradox: cells perceive the drop back to normoxia as hypoxia, activating HIF-1alpha, VEGF, mobilizing CD34+ stem cells, and lengthening telomeres.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Hachmo, Efrati et al. (Aging 2020): 60 daily 90-minute sessions at 2.0 ATA with air breaks extended leukocyte telomere length by >20% and reduced senescent T-cells by 37% in aging human volunteers.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 55%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Barotrauma to tympanic membrane or sinuses; central nervous system oxygen toxicity if pressure exceeds limits; high financial cost.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Protocolized series of 40-60 daily sessions (90 minutes at 1.5-2.0 ATA with 100% O2 and 5-minute air breaks) conducted periodically.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Middle-ear barotrauma, myopia shifts, or lack of telomere length elongation.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
