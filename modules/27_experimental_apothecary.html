1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

7,8-DHF acts as a direct small-molecule TrkB agonist (BDNF mimic). Selbex (Teprenone) induces systemic HSP70. Dopa Mucuna provides L-DOPA substrate. 1,3-DMHA is a potent TAAR1 sympathomimetic amine releasing dopamine and norepinephrine.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Preclinical rodent data on 7,8-DHF restoring spatial memory; clinical gastro-protective trials on Teprenone HSP70 chaperoning; sports science literature on sympathomimetics.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Lethal hypertensive interaction: 1,3-DMHA must NEVER be co-administered with Selegiline (MAO-B inhibitor). Dopa Mucuna must be strictly cycled to prevent tyrosine hydroxylase down-regulation.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

7,8-DHF (25mg sublingual); Selbex (50mg post-thermal stress); Dopa Mucuna (cycled 1-2x/week); 1,3-DMHA (strictly reserved for rare athletic competition, zero selegiline on board).

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
