Taurine: Mitochondrial tRNA Translation & Osmoregulation

Pillar: Metabolic Substrate Flexibility & Mitochondrial Health
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E4 (Murine Lifespan) / E3 (Human Cross-Sectional)
Stance: Consensus-Adjacent (Readily available; underappreciated organellar role)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $8 -- $12 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Mitochondrial Dysfunction & Loss of Proteostasis

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Serves as substrate for MTO1/GTPBP3 mitochondrial tRNA-Leu taurinomethylation at nucleotide 34, forming 5-taurinomethyluridine (tau-m5-U). Stabilizes UUG codon translation of 13 mtDNA-encoded hydrophobic Complex I subunits (ND6, ND1). Stabilizes SERCA2a calcium ATPase in muscle, acts as intracellular osmolyte, and conjugates secondary bile acids (taurocholate) activating TGR5.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Singh et al. (Science 2023): Circulating taurine declines >80% with age in humans and primates. Oral supplementation in mice increased median lifespan by 10-12% and life expectancy at 28 months by 18-25%. Improved bone mineral density, muscle endurance, and reduced cellular senescence.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 60%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Exceptionally safe up to 6g daily. Human plasma levels do not always mirror intracellular muscle/brain concentrations. High doses may compete with beta-alanine for the TauT (SLC6A6) transporter.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

3g to 6g daily of pure pharmaceutical-grade powder dissolved in water. Take 3g morning fasted or divided 3g AM / 3g PM post-workout. Synergy with Creatine Monohydrate.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Renal decline (Cystatin-C eGFR drop >15%), or emerging human trials proving plasma taurine is purely a passive bystander of chronological age with zero cellular benefit.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.

7. Epistemic Frontier Questions (For Advanced AI Interrogation)

Is human TauT (SLC6A6) transporter expression down-regulated in aging cardiomyocytes and neurons, and does taurine prodrug design (e.g. lipophilic esters) achieve higher mitochondrial matrix partitioning than oral free taurine?
