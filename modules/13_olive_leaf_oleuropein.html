Olive Leaf Extract: Oleuropein, oxLDL Shielding & Endothelial eNOS

Pillar: Cardiovascular Longevity & Blood Pressure Control
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E2 (Human Active-Controlled Blood Pressure Trials)
Stance: Consensus-Adjacent (Herbal cardio-protective with pharmaceutical-grade data)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $12 -- $16 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Mitochondrial Dysfunction & Chronic Inflammation

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Rich in the secoiridoid glycoside Oleuropein and its metabolite Hydroxytyrosol. Chelates catalytic free iron and copper ions in circulating lipoproteins, preventing the lipid peroxidation that transforms ApoB into atherogenic oxLDL. Stimulates eNOS phosphorylation and acts as a natural ACE-inhibitor.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Susalit et al. (Phytomedicine 2011): 500mg standardized extract twice daily reduced systolic blood pressure by -11.5 mmHg and diastolic by -4.8 mmHg in stage-1 hypertensive patients, matching 10mg Captopril head-to-head.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 75%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Mild additive hypotensive effect when combined with tadalafil or antihypertensives. Extremely well tolerated.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

500mg daily of standardized Olive Leaf Extract (20% Oleuropein) taken with breakfast or lunch.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Persistent hypotension or absence of blood pressure stabilizing effects.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
