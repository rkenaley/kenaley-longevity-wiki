1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Water-soluble organosulfur compounds, predominantly S-allylcysteine (SAC) and S-allylmercaptocysteine. Scavenges reactive oxygen and nitrogen species, suppresses macrophage oxidized LDL uptake, inhibits HMG-CoA reductase, and lowers vascular inflammation.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Budoff et al. (J Nutr 2016): Randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled trial in patients with metabolic syndrome demonstrated that 1200mg Kyolic daily significantly retarded CAC progression and induced volumetric regression of low-attenuation (vulnerable soft) plaque.
