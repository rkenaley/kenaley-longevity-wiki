Vitamin K2 (MK-7): Matrix Gla Protein Carboxylation & Arterial Decalcification

Pillar: Cardiovascular Longevity & Bone Homeostasis

Evidence Grade: E2 (VitaK-CAC Trial) / E3 (Rotterdam Cohort)
Stance: Consensus-Adjacent (Recognized in bone/vascular health; dose-critical)

Primary Target Hallmark: Loss of Proteostasis & Intercellular Communication

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Acts as obligatory enzymatic cofactor for gamma-glutamyl carboxylase, transforming uncarboxylated Matrix Gla Protein (ucMGP) into carboxylated MGP (cMGP). Active cMGP chelates free calcium phosphate in the vascular media and intima, preventing medial elastocalcinosis and aortic stiffness.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Rotterdam Study (Geleijnse 2004): High dietary menaquinone intake correlated with 52% reduction in severe aortic calcification and 57% lower coronary mortality. VitaK-CAC Trial (Zwakenberg 2021) demonstrated arrest of arterial pulse wave velocity acceleration.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 75%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Contraindicated with vitamin K antagonist anticoagulants (Warfarin). Completely safe with direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs) and antiplatelets.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

100mcg to 200mcg Menaquinone-7 (MK-7) daily taken with a fat-containing meal (prolonged circulatory half-life of ~72 hours).

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Coronary artery calcium (CAC) score accelerating faster than age-expected progression (>15% per year), or hypercalcemia.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.

7. Epistemic Frontier Questions (For Advanced AI Interrogation)
