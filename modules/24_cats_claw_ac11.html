Cat's Claw (AC-11): Carboxy Alkyl Esters, NF-kappaB & PARP DNA Repair

Pillar: Epigenetics & Genomic Stability
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E3 (Human Volunteer DNA Repair Trials)
Stance: Contrarian (Standardized aqueous extract vs crude unstandardized bark)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $22 -- $28 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Genomic Instability & Chronic Inflammation

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Standardized aqueous extract of Uncaria tomentosa rich in Carboxy Alkyl Esters (CAEs). Inhibits NF-kappaB nuclear translocation by preventing IkappaBalpha phosphorylation. Upregulates Poly(ADP-Ribose) Polymerase (PARP) activity and stimulates Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER) of DNA lesions.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Sheng, Pero et al. (Phytomedicine 2000, 2001): Human clinical trials demonstrated that oral AC-11 significantly enhanced the repair of single- and double-strand DNA damage induced by radiation/chemotherapy and normalized leukocyte survival.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 65%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Avoid in acute autoimmune disease flares or organ transplant recipients due to immune enhancement. Very safe toxicology profile.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

350mg to 700mg daily standardized AC-11 taken with the morning stack.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: hs-CRP failing to normalize (<0.5 mg/L), or gastrointestinal intolerance.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
