Albumin & Mineral Reconstitution Protocol Post-Apheresis

Pillar: Hematological Cleansing & Hepatic Protein Synthesis
Conviction Tier: TESTING / EXPERIMENTAL CYCLE
Evidence Grade: E6 (Physiological Kinetics) / E7 (Empirical Protocol)
Stance: Contrarian (Active physiological chaperoning vs passive saline recovery)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $15 -- $20 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Loss of Proteostasis & Intercellular Communication

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Normal human liver synthesizes 10.5-15g of albumin daily (half-life 19 days). Post-donation recovery requires rapid delivery of rate-limiting amino acid substrates (cysteine, methionine, BCAAs) and immediate replenishment of ionized calcium and magnesium bound by citrate.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Rothschild et al. (Hepatology 1988) on hepatic albumin synthesis regulation by osmotic pressure and nutritional substrate availability. Standard clinical apheresis citrate toxicity management guidelines.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 80%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Overconsumption of calcium citrate causing gastrointestinal upset; failure to replenish salt leading to orthostatic hypotension.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Hours 0-2: 500mg elemental calcium + 200mg magnesium bisglycinate + 2g sea salt in 1L water. Hours 1-6: 15g free-form EAAs followed by 35-40g whey isolate. Days 1-2: 1200mg NAC + 3g Glycine + 300mg Silymarin (milk thistle).

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Post-apheresis paresthesias (hypocalcemia) or failure of serum albumin to return to >4.5 g/dL within 7 days.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
