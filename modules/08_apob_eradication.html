ApoB Eradication Stack: PCSK9 Inhibition, Ezetimibe & Citrus Bergamot

Pillar: Cardiovascular Longevity & Endothelial Glycocalyx
Conviction Tier: CORE BET
Evidence Grade: E1 (FOURIER / Hard Outcomes) / E2 (Bergamot Biomarkers)
Stance: Contrarian on Target (<40-50 mg/dL ApoB vs standard clinical <80-90 mg/dL)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $16/mo (Bergamot) -- $250+/mo (PCSK9i)
Primary Target Hallmark: Altered Intercellular Communication & Cellular Senescence

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Suppresses circulating ApoB particles to primitive neonatal levels (<40-50 mg/dL), rendering subendothelial atheroma initiation biophysically impossible. PCSK9 mAbs prevent LDLR lysosomal degradation; Ezetimibe blocks NPC1L1 sterol absorption; Citrus Bergamot inhibits HMG-CoA reductase and stimulates AMPK.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

FOURIER Trial (Sabatine 2017 NEJM): Evolocumab lowered LDL-C to median 30 mg/dL, driving significant 15-20% MACE reductions with progressive benefit down to <20 mg/dL and zero neurocognitive harm. GLAGOV IVUS trial proved plaque regression.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 90%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Extremely high cost if using brand PCSK9i without insurance. Ezetimibe and Bergamot are cheap and benign. No evidence of cognitive or steroidogenic harm at ultra-low ApoB.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Citrus Bergamot 500-1000mg standardized extract daily + Ezetimibe 10mg daily. Escalate to Evolocumab (Repatha 140mg SQ bi-weekly) if baseline ApoB remains >60 mg/dL.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: ApoB remaining >60 mg/dL, or cognitive memory decline on computerized testing.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.

7. Epistemic Frontier Questions (For Advanced AI Interrogation)

Is there any physiological lower limit to circulating ApoB in adult humans below which steroidogenesis, fat-soluble vitamin shuttling, or membrane lipid turnover is impaired?

v5.0 Kinetic & Stoichiometric Optimization Patch

  • Hepatic SREBP-2 Rebound Blockade: Augment or replace Citrus Bergamot with Bempedoic Acid (180 mg) alongside Ezetimibe (10 mg) and PCSK9 inhibition. Bempedoic acid is a prodrug activated exclusively by hepatic ACSVL1 (absent in skeletal muscle), inhibiting ATP-citrate lyase (ACLY) with zero myotoxicity while reducing hs-CRP by ~25% (directly lowering DunedinPACE).
