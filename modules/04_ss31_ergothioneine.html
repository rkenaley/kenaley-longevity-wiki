1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

The inner mitochondrial membrane (IMM) exhibits extreme negative curvature at cristae junctions, stabilized by the cone-shaped non-bilayer phospholipid tetralinoleoyl cardiolipin (\text{TLCL}). With age and oxidative flux:
1. Cardiolipin acyl chains undergo peroxidation at bis-allylic carbons, disrupting the electrostatic anchoring of Cytochrome c to Complex III and Complex IV.
2. Detached Cytochrome c undergoes a conformational transition (breaking the \text{Fe-Met80} coordination bond), converting it from an electron shuttle into a cardiolipin-specific peroxidase that amplifies IMM lipid peroxidation, flattens cristae, opens the mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP), and induces reverse electron transport (RET) superoxide generation at Complex I.

A. SS-31 (Elamipretide / D-Arg-Dmt-Lys-Phe-\text{NH}_2)

  • Electrostatic & Hydrophobic Partitioning: SS-31 is an alternating aromatic-cationic tetrapeptide that crosses the outer mitochondrial membrane and concentrates $1,000\text{--}5,000\times exclusively on the IMM in a \Delta\Psi_m-independent manner.
  • Piezoelectric & Cristae Curvature Restoration: The cationic D-Arg and Lys residues form salt bridges with the dianionic phosphate headgroups of cardiolipin, while the 2',6'-dimethyltyrosine (Dmt) and Phe aromatic rings insert into the interfacial acyl region. This sterically blocks the \text{Fe-Met80} rupture in Cytochrome c, abolishes cardiolipin peroxidase activity, tightens respirasome supercomplex (\text{CI} + \text{CIII}_2 + \text{CIV}_n) packing, and restores the mitochondrial contact site and cristae organizing system (MICOS) without quenching physiological hormetic signaling.
  • Epigenetic & Functional Rejuvenation: Validated to reverse age-related cardiac diastolic dysfunction, restore skeletal muscle \text{ATP}/\text{O}_2 coupling (P/O ratio), and rescue glomerular podocyte mitochondrial architecture (preserving eGFR, a primary DunedinPACE denominator).

B. L-Ergothioneine (SLC22A4 / OCTN1 Cytoprotective Thione)

  • Tautomeric Stability: Unlike glutathione (\text{GSH}) or lipophilic \text{C}_{60} fullerenes, L-Ergothioneine exists predominantly in the thione tautomer at physiological pH (E_0' = -0.06\text{ V}), preventing spontaneous autoxidation or Fenton-like pro-oxidant radical generation in the presence of free \text{Fe}^{2+} or \text{Cu}^{2+}.
  • Targeted Mitochondrial Accumulation: Transported selectively by the carnitine/organic cation transporter OCTN1 (SLC22A4) directly into erythrocytes, bone marrow hematopoietic progenitor cells, ocular lens, and mitochondria, where it scavenges singlet oxygen (^1\text{O}_2), peroxynitrite (\text{ONOO}^-), and hypochlorous acid (\text{HOCl}) while exhibiting zero phototoxicity under 660/850 nm irradiation (30) or 2.0 ATA hyperoxia (29).

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

  • Grohn et al. (GeroScience 2021): Proved Baati C60 in olive oil fails to extend mammalian lifespan and exhibits light-dependent phototoxicity, necessitating replacement.
  • Campbell et al. (JCI Insight 2019) / Sweetwyne et al. (Aging Cell 2017): 8-week SS-31 administration in 24-month-old mice reverses age-related cardiac hypertrophy, restores diastolic function, and rescues skeletal muscle mitochondrial energetics to young-adult levels.
  • Paul & Snyder (Cell Death Differ 2010) / Cheah & Halliwell (FEBS Lett 2012): Establishes OCTN1-dependent cellular accumulation of ergothioneine as an essential physiologic tissue radioprotectant.
