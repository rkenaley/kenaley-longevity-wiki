Urolithin A: Gut Microbial Metabolite & PINK1/Parkin Mitophagy

Pillar: Mitochondrial Dynamics & Quality Control
Conviction Tier: WATCHLIST / TARGETED ADJUNCT
Evidence Grade: E2 (Human RCTs on Muscle Endurance)
Stance: Consensus-Supported (Validated in peer-reviewed clinical trials)
Estimated Monthly Cost: $25 -- $45 / month
Primary Target Hallmark: Mitochondrial Dysfunction & Disabled Macroautophagy

1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Microbiome-derived metabolite of ellagitannins (from pomegranates and walnuts). Crosses cellular membranes to activate PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy, clearing depolarized mitochondria and stimulating skeletal muscle mitochondrial volume density.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Andreux et al. (Nat Metab 2019): Demonstrated safety and human mitochondrial gene expression signatures. Liu et al. (JAMA Netw Open 2022): 1000mg Urolithin A significantly increased muscle endurance (6-minute walk distance) in older adults. Singh et al. (Nat Aging 2022).

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 70%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

High retail cost of synthetic Urolithin A (Mitopure). Only ~30-40% of humans have the gut microbiome metabotype (Gordonibacter urolithinfaciens) to convert dietary ellagitannins into active urolithin A.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

500mg to 1000mg daily of direct synthetic Urolithin A, or 500mg standardized Pomegranate Ellagitannin extract for confirmed producers.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Failure to observe subjective recovery benefits or objective muscle endurance improvements after 90 days.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
