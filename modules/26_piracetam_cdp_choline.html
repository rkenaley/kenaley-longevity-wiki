1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Piracetam restores lost mitochondrial membrane fluidity in aging neurons, modulates AMPA receptor density, and enhances microvascular cerebral blood flow. Alpha-GPC (L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine) serves as a bioavailable choline donor, guaranteeing acetylcholine synthesis.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Extensive clinical literature in European neurology establishing efficacy in post-stroke recovery, mild cognitive impairment, and cortical oxygen utilization.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 65%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Alpha-GPC has been associated with modest elevations in trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) in large Korean observational cohorts; monitor plasma TMAO or cycle.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

1600mg Piracetam + 300mg Alpha-GPC taken pre-workout or during demanding cognitive deep-work blocks.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Headaches (choline deficiency sign) or worsening of vascular inflammatory markers.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
