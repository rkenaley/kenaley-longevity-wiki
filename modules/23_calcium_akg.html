1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Alpha-ketoglutarate is an obligatory co-substrate for 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenases (2-OGDDs), including TET1-3 DNA demethylases and Jumonji-C histone demethylases. Supplementation overcomes age-associated competitive inhibition by succinate and fumarate, rescuing active DNA demethylation and restoring youthful chromatin accessibility.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Asadi Shahmirzadi et al. (Cell Metab 2020): Calcium-AKG extended female mouse lifespan by 12% and reduced clinical frailty by >40%. Human observational data (Rejuvant trial) demonstrated significant reductions in biological age clocks.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 70%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Extremely safe endogenous metabolite. Calcium salt provides elemental calcium; monitor total dietary calcium intake.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

1000mg to 1500mg Calcium-AKG daily taken in the morning.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Hypercalcemia or lack of improvement in subjective energy and epigenetic methylation clocks after 12 months.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
