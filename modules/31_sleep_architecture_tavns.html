1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

During NREM Stage 3 slow-wave sleep, astrocytic aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels facilitate convective cerebrospinal fluid flushing through the brain parenchyma, clearing amyloid-beta and tau. Nasal mouth taping forces continuous nasal airflow, stimulating paranasal nitric oxide synthesis and stabilizing blood oxygen saturation.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Nedergaard laboratory glymphatic clearance papers; clinical studies on medical micropore mouth taping demonstrating significant reductions in snoring, sleep apnea index (AHI), and oral dysbiosis.

  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Contraindicated during severe nasal congestion or acute rhinitis. Medical-grade hypoallergenic micropore tape avoids skin irritation.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Apply 3M micropore tape horizontally over lips before sleep. Maintain complete blackout darkness and a 3-hour pre-bed fast.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria
