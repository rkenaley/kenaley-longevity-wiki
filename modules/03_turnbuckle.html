1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Proposes shifting the Inner Mitochondrial Membrane (IMM) from an uncoupled, proton-leaking state to an up-latched state via cardiolipin coupling and ATP synthase dimer stabilization. Uses pulsed nicotinamide (NAM) to trigger Drp1 fission and mitophagy of mutant mtDNA, followed by hyper-fusion via PQQ and CoQ10 to promote symmetric stem-cell renewal.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Dischler Patents: US 11,504,351 B2 (Issued 2022) and US 2025/0221952 A1. Relies on preclinical biophysical models of respiratory supercomplex coupling, though lacks formal independent double-blind mammalian lifespan trials.

3. Honest Bayesian Probability & Expected Value

  • P(Real Human Healthspan Extension): 35%
  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

High-dose nicotinamide (1.5g) can cause flushing, transient sirtuin feedback inhibition, and elevated homocysteine via methyl depletion. Excessive mitochondrial stimulation can prematurely drive stem cell differentiation.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Quarterly execution: Phase 1 (Days 1-2): 1.5g Nicotinamide in divided doses to induce deep fission/mitophagy. Phase 2 (Days 3-7): High-dose PQQ (20-40mg) + CoQ10 (200mg) + Taurine (6g) to lock into hyper-fused symmetric expansion.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Elevation of resting blood lactate (>2.0 mmol/L), decline in fasted Zone 2 power output, or worsening of DNA methylation clocks (DunedinPACE).
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
