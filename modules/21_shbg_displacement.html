1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Eurycomanone (Tongkat Ali) stimulates Leydig cell steroidogenesis and suppresses cortisol. Urtica dioica root lignans ((-)-3,4-divanillyltetrahydrofuran) bind the steroid-binding pocket of SHBG with high affinity, competitively displacing testosterone into active free testosterone. Boron citrate downregulates hepatic SHBG transcription.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Talbott et al. (JISSN 2013): Tongkat Ali improved stress hormones and elevated free testosterone. Naghii et al. (J Trace Elem Med Biol 2011): 10mg boron daily significantly lowered SHBG and increased free testosterone within 7 days.
