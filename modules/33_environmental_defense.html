1. Biophysical & Biochemical Mechanism

Captures fine airborne particulate matter (PM2.5) that directly triggers endothelial glycocalyx degradation and systemic pulmonary cytokine release. Bulk activated carbon adsorbs volatile organic compounds (VOCs). Reverse osmosis removes microplastics, PFAS, and endocrine-disrupting heavy metals.

2. The Empirical Evidence Base

Cardiovascular and pulmonary epidemiology consistently proving that high indoor PM2.5 levels accelerate arterial plaque progression and elevate hs-CRP; filtration trials demonstrate improved flow-mediated dilation.

  • Expected Value Justification: The intervention targets a fundamental rate-limiting step in biological aging. The asymmetric upside significantly outweighs operational friction and monetary expense.

4. Physiological Failure Modes & Drug-Drug Interactions

Zero physiological risks; failure to replace saturated carbon filters can lead to desorption.

5. Precise Operational Protocol

Continuous True HEPA (H13/H14) + bulk activated carbon filtration in bedroom and workspace; multi-stage reverse osmosis water with remineralization.

6. Objective Surveillance & Kill Criteria

  • Biomarkers to Monitor: Indoor PM2.5 exceeding 5 ug/m^3 on optical laser air quality monitors.
  • Kill Rule: If biomarkers cross negative thresholds or objective efficacy is absent after the designated evaluation window, terminate or down-titrate the bet immediately.
